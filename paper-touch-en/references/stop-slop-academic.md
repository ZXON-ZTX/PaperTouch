# Stop Slop: academic adaptation for expert mode

Use this reference within explicitly selected expert mode when prose contains empty framing, formulaic presentation, vague referents or promotional jargon. It does not add E1–E6 triggers, change default mode, authorize substantive revision, or require an additional editing pass. The shared scope and scientific-content contract in [expert-editing.md](expert-editing.md), the relevant field profile and supplied author/venue preferences remain controlling.

The source is Hardik Pandya's Stop Slop skill and its phrases, structures and examples references. Its general-copywriting prohibitions are adapted below; they are not imported as an academic word blacklist. Source and [MIT notice](stop-slop-LICENSE.txt) are recorded in [source-notes.md](source-notes.md#stop-slop-academic-adaptation-2026-11-03).

## Useful editorial actions

| Located problem | Smallest useful action | Keep or skip when |
|---|---|---|
| A framing shell announces a point without contributing an assertion or relation, such as `Here's the thing:` or a standalone `Let that sink in.` | Remove the empty framing and retain the complete statement, necessary punctuation and its scientific scope. | The wording is quoted, author-required, conveys a warning/attribution or introduces a real question. Do not classify `In this section` or every section roadmap as empty without checking its navigational purpose. |
| A rhetorical question, reveal or fragment merely restates the immediately supplied point. | State that same point directly, or join its fragments locally when every assertion remains. | The question is a research question, hypothesis, experimental comparison, caption/heading or a genuine unresolved question. Preserve lists and paragraph structure. |
| Repeated emphatic phrases or promotional jargon obscure a function already stated in the same context. | Express the existing action or relation in ordinary academic language; remove only redundant emphasis. | A phrase is a defined term, a substantive author judgment, a necessary qualifier or carries measurable meaning. `Leverage score`, `simply connected` and `significantly` with statistical meaning are not filler. |
| A vague pronoun or declarative refers to something identifiable in the supplied context. | Name that existing referent or explain the existing implication with its original certainty and attribution. | Resolving it would require guessing a mechanism, result, cause, population, actor or significance. Keep the claim rather than deleting it or manufacturing detail; flag the precise gap only when material and compatible with the requested output. |
| An action is assigned to an unclear actor and the source already identifies the responsible entity. | Clarify the entity and its action without changing agency or research status. Active `we`, appropriate passive voice and system subjects are all available. | A passive construction properly emphasizes the procedure, the actor is unknown, or the subject is a legitimate mathematical/technical entity. A solver minimizes an objective and an algorithm converges; do not invent a person to satisfy a style rule. |
| A quotable closing line or dramatic fragment repeats an assertion already stated without adding information. | Remove or integrate the repeated packaging while keeping all original claims and logical relations. | The sentence states an independent conclusion, limitation, caveat, negative finding or required disclosure. A short sentence is not a trigger by itself. |

Directness means making the same author claim easier to read. It does not mean stronger certainty. If an apparently empty word changes the truth conditions, attribution, scope or emphasis required by the author, preserve it. Do not remove a claim solely because its supporting evidence was not supplied.

## Academic exceptions to the original prohibitions

- **Adverbs and hedges:** do not ban `-ly` words. Keep randomization (`randomly assigned`), convergence (`almost surely`), statistical meaning (`statistically significant`), approximation, frequency, locality and other scope/certainty markers. Remove a modifier only when it is genuinely redundant and its full meaning survives.
- **Voice and agency:** do not require a human subject in every sentence. Preserve valid methods passives, mathematical statements, system operations and accurate non-human subjects. Do not silently attribute an experiment to the authors or add `you` or an invented scene.
- **Negation and contrast:** keep real exclusions, negative findings, controls, comparison groups and distinctions such as metadata versus plaintext. A `not X but Y` shape alone is not grounds to discard X. Flatten rhetorical packaging only when no independent information is lost.
- **Questions and conditionals:** preserve genuine research questions, hypotheses, `When` conditions and their timing/causal meaning. A Wh-word at the start of a sentence is not a defect.
- **Lists, rhythm and punctuation:** keep technical three-item lists, necessary long sentences, deliberate author punctuation, ranges, minus signs and term hyphens. Do not delete an em dash merely because it occurs or alter sentence lengths to manufacture irregularity. Improve rhythm only as a consequence of fixing a real clarity problem.
- **Reader orientation:** clarify referents and the existing reasoning, without turning a formal paper into conversational second-person copy. Preserve the author's genre and voice rather than imposing intimacy or distance rules.

## Review and finish

Use directness, clarity, warranted certainty, referent precision and useful information density as qualitative questions about an actual proposed edit. Do not assign an authenticity score, claim human authorship, import the source's 35/50 revision threshold or keep rewriting to reach an arbitrary score. The source examples are general prose illustrations, not authority to drop `most`, convert a qualified finding to a universal one or insert a fact into a manuscript.

Check the final text against the original for propositions, negative findings, numbers, units, notation, references, attribution and research state. Keep clear passages unchanged. Audit-only requests produce findings without a replacement manuscript; edited-text-only requests receive only the requested text. Reuse the existing comparison/checks, and stop when the selected scope is complete. The E1–E6 patch helper still cannot execute these broader expert edits.
