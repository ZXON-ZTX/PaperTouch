An English translation of the documentation is not an English validation corpus. The English version here inherits the editing contract, not the Chinese feature ratios, thresholds or a claim of detector performance.

## What is retained

Explicit locatable rules; minimum necessary changes; exact non-match preservation; information conservation; original document structure; author-style exceptions; no fabricated specificity; an internal rule-to-edit check before output. Required disclosures and scientific content remain intact.

## What is changed

Chinese enumeration commas, Chinese translationese and zero-subject comments are not translated into English bans. Genuine English `When` clauses, passive methods, research questions, formal nominalizations, parallel technical lists, numbered sections and real contrasts remain legitimate. Em dashes/colons are not automatically removed. The six English operations in english-rules.md are a deliberately narrow editorial whitelist, not empirically established authorship markers.

Unlike a broad academic-polishing skill, this version does not automatically repair scientific overclaims, invent supporting data, reorganize paragraphs or substitute technical vocabulary. A concern outside E1–E6 stays unchanged unless a separately authorized review is requested. Broader proofreading and substantive editing should be treated as separate tasks with their own acceptance criteria.

## Verification boundary

The patch helper checks some mechanical invariants. Model-based synthetic editing cases check rule use and exceptions. Neither proves scientific validity, complete format support, human authorship, a lower AI-detection rate or a similarity percentage. No commercial detector, external service or large English corpus evaluation is part of this version.

## Optional expert mode (2026-10-21)

The original six-rule whitelist and patch helper are unchanged. Corpus acquisition/phrase analysis, selected section reading and held-out applicability checks are not a human/AI authorship experiment or a detector evaluation. Paper PDFs and full extracted text are not redistributed; original paper copyrights remain with their respective holders. The earlier verification boundary above describes the whitelist version; the added study's actual scope is stated in its own record.
